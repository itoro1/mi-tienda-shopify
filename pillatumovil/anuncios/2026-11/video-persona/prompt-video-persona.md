# Vídeo con persona · Pillatumovil (borrador para corregir)

## Idea
Una dependienta joven y simpática, en una tienda elegante con el cartel LED «Pillatumovil», saca las dos cajas reales y habla a cámara.
Es la vendedora de la tienda, no un cliente. Así no es una opinión inventada, que la ley no permite.

## Guion (15 segundos)
1. **0-3 s · gancho.** Con el iPhone en la mano: «¿Su iPhone vino sin cargador? Normal, Apple ya no lo pone».
2. **3-7 s.** Pone las dos cajas en el mostrador: «Aquí tiene los dos: adaptador de 20 vatios y cable. Originales de Apple, precintados».
3. **7-11 s.** Sonrisa: «En Apple le cuestan 38 euros. Aquí, 18,99. Compramos por cantidad y ganamos menos».
4. **11-15 s.** Señala abajo, al botón: «Envío gratis, en 24 o 48 horas en su casa. Hasta el jueves. Toque "Comprar"».

Los textos en pantalla los pongo yo después: el precio, «En Apple, 38,00 € (precio del 01/11/2026)» y el botón pillatumovil.com.

## Prompt para el generador
```
Vertical 9:16 UGC-style ad, 15 seconds, native audio, Spanish from Spain.

CHARACTER: a friendly Spanish shop assistant, woman in her late 20s, long
dark-brown hair loosely tied back, natural everyday makeup, small gold hoop
earrings, plain black crew-neck t-shirt, warm natural smile, confident and
relaxed, talks straight to camera like a FaceTime call.
She is the store's salesperson, NOT a customer giving a review.

SETTING: an upscale modern phone boutique at evening. Warm wood counter,
soft-white back wall with a glowing neon LED sign reading "Pillatumovil".
Out-of-focus shelves behind her with several iPhone models on display stands,
including large titanium Pro Max models. Soft key light from the left, warm
practical lights, shallow depth of field, premium but real — like a real
shop filmed on a new iPhone, not a 3D render.

PRODUCT (use reference images exactly, do not redesign): two white Apple
retail boxes — one "20W USB-C Power Adapter" box and one cable box. Keep the
real box artwork, Apple logo and labels exactly as in the references; boxes
sealed, standing upright on the counter, never floating.

BEATS:
0-3s  Handheld close-up, she holds an iPhone, raises eyebrows and says:
      "¿Su iPhone vino sin cargador? Normal, Apple ya no lo pone."
3-7s  Quick push-in, she places both boxes on the counter, taps each one:
      "Aquí tiene los dos: adaptador de 20 vatios y cable. Originales de
      Apple, precintados."
7-11s Medium shot, she leans in with a half smile:
      "En Apple le cuestan 38 euros. Aquí, 18,99. Compramos por cantidad
      y ganamos menos."
11-15s She points down toward the bottom of the frame:
      "Envío gratis, en 24 o 48 horas en su casa. Hasta el jueves.
      Toque 'Comprar'."

CAMERA: handheld, natural micro-movements, one fast cut per beat, crisp
focus on face and boxes. AUDIO: clear close-mic voice, light shop ambience,
no music under the voice.

DO NOT: no on-screen text or subtitles (added later), no other brands, no
fake reviews or "I bought it", no Apple Store look, no extra logos, no
distorted hands, boxes must not change shape or text between shots.
```
